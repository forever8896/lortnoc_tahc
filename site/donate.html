What this pays for

Natural Language Encryption runs on one warm machine, because reversibility needs both ends on identical distributions. That machine is the cost. It does not scale per message and it does not bill more under load — it queues, and it slows.

Everything else here is time: the extension, the contracts, the audit, the tests that stop us shipping something that quietly fails in your hands.

This buys nothing. It does not move you up the alpha queue, it is not a pre-order, and it grants no handle, no account and no access of any kind. If you want in, the alpha form is free and always will be. We would rather have your bug reports than your money.
