Chrome, Brave, Edge and Arc · SHA-256 2709390f…5e5f79

  1. Check the download. The hash must match, and GitHub confirms our CI built it.
    shasum -a 256 lortnoc-tahc-everywhere-ew-v0.2.4.zip
# 2709390f283a771ad80b895c36eda99282891607e92b902c3557f8744f5e5f79
gh attestation verify lortnoc-tahc-everywhere-ew-v0.2.4.zip -R forever8896/lortnoc_tahc
  2. Load it. Unzip it somewhere it can stay, open chrome://extensions, switch on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  3. Add your keys. Open the extension and press the gear: World ID, a wallet, or a passphrase you were given.
  4. Write and read. Press Ctrl+Shift+L in any text box to write. Press Find hidden posts to read, or turn on Always on for a site.
