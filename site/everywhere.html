How it works

A message that says nothing about itself.

01 · WRITE

Pick who can read it

Press Ctrl+Shift+L in any text box. Write your message, choose readers: a passphrase, verified humans, citizens of a country, holders of an NFT space, a date — or combine them.

02 · POST

It becomes ordinary words

Your message is encrypted, then turned into plain chatter. No tag, no marker, no rule in the clear — nobody can tell it's a message, or who it's for.

03 · READ

Keys open what's yours

Readers connect their keys once — passphrases, World ID, a wallet. Posts meant for them open by themselves; everything else stays an ordinary post.

Install · 2 minutes

Get it running

  1. Download lortnoc-tahc-everywhere-ew-v0.2.0.zip and check it: 
    shasum -a 256 lortnoc-tahc-everywhere-ew-v0.2.0.zip

gh attestation verify lortnoc-tahc-everywhere-ew-v0.2.0.zip -R forever8896/lortnoc_tahc
  2. Unzip it somewhere it can stay, open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode.
  3. Load unpacked → pick the unzipped folder. Pin the extension.
  4. Add your keys: the extension button → ⚙ → Keys — a passphrase you were given, World ID (verified human, or your passport's nationality), a wallet.
  5. Write: click any text box, press Ctrl+Shift+L. Read: extension button → Find hidden posts — or switch on Always on for a site and it checks every page for you.

Honest limits

What it doesn't do

  • The gate operator holds the key pieces for time, World ID and NFT rules. Add a passphrase to a rule and the gate alone can't read the post.
  • The gate learns which of its posts your page showed when your extension asks what you may open.
  • Cover text can look machine-written to a trained detector. This hides who a post is for and that it's ours from casual and keyword censorship — not from a determined analyst.
  • A guessable passphrase can be guessed. The generated five words cannot.
  • ENS v2 lives on Sepolia until it reaches mainnet; space payments are on Ethereum mainnet.
