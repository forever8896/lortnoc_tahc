ENS v2 — live on-chain proof

This page reads our ENS v2 records straight from Sepolia in your browser, right now, and shows every raw JSON-RPC call it makes. Nothing is stored here; point it at any Sepolia RPC you trust.

Why our names don't show on app.ens.domains. We use ENS v2 — the new, experimental Sepolia deployment (ensdomains/contracts-v2, snapshot 2026-06-29). The consumer ENS app and most explorers only index ENS v1, so kilian.lortnoctahc.eth won't appear there. That's expected for v2, not a sign it isn't real. The records are on-chain; this page reads them at the contract level, the same way any ENS v2 client will.
How resolution works here (two live calls per handle).
1.
LortnocRegistry.getResolver(label) → the handle's own resolver contract. Our registry is a real ENS v2 IRegistry slotted under lortnoctahc.eth.
2.
resolver.text(namehash, key) → the record value written on-chain (messaging key, Sui pointer, knock challenge…).
Both are plain eth_call reads — no wallet, no trust in us. Two handles returning two different resolver addresses is the proof that every handle gets its own resolver, not a shared row.
