Gate

Holds the key pieces for time, World ID and NFT rules, and hands them only to readers who qualify.

You write. In the extension, never in the page. Your message is encrypted under the rule you pick — a passphrase, verified humans, a nationality, holders of an NFT, a date, or a mix.

It becomes ordinary words. The encrypted bytes are turned into plain chatter. Nothing marks it as a message — not even which rule it uses.

Readers connect their keys once. Passphrases, World ID, wallets. Their extension quietly tries them on every post.

Only posts meant for them open. For everyone else, it stays an ordinary post.

Honest limits: whoever runs the gate holds its key pieces — add a passphrase to a rule and the gate alone can't read it. A guessable passphrase can be guessed; the generated five words cannot.
